Obligations

The node's own append-only ledger. Opening, approving and closing all go through its own methods — this panel and those acts read and write the same file, so they cannot disagree.

A symbolic reference is safest. A path-like one must actually resolve on this machine, or the ledger refuses it — a citation is never written false.
A material obligation cannot be closed until it has cleared the breath-gate, and the ledger refuses to approve one with no human gate behind it. Leave it off for routine work.
