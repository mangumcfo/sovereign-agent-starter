# The Uncapturable Constellation — Strategic Positioning (July 2026)

Slide deck: [`USN_CONSTELLATION_POSITIONING_2026-07.pdf`](USN_CONSTELLATION_POSITIONING_2026-07.pdf)
(17 slides, 1120×630).

**What it is.** The strategic positioning of a distributed constellation of
lower-powered, low-latency Universal Sovereign Nodes alongside the 2026 frontier-AI
landscape. The thesis: the constellation does not need to match frontier intelligence
node-for-node — it competes on resilience, local sovereignty, attestation, and
uncapturability, and open-source models are its **collaborative substrate**, not its
rivals. The risk named throughout is capture *dynamics* (serving concentration,
jurisdiction, license flips, distribution chokepoints) — never the models or the
people who make them.

**Honesty posture.** Every capability claim in the deck is graded (runs today /
staged / designed / parked) against this repository's actual code and test suite at
the time of writing; the deck deliberately leads its own positioning matrix with the
two axes where it loses. Nothing in it is an offer, a token sale, or a claim of
deployed federation.

**Provenance.** Produced under the house production line: grounded fact-dig →
build under machine acceptance (sequential footers, banned-claims sweep, status-chip
honesty check) → independent adversarial review → human word on framing. The seal
line stays human, as everywhere in this project.
